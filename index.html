Izakaya playbook — learn it tonight, use it all trip
  • “Toriaezu, nama de” — “draft beer to start”; the universal opener while you think
  • Otoshi — the small dish that appears unasked; it’s a seating charge, not a mistake
  • “Osusume wa?” — “what do you recommend?” — the best menu in any room
  • “Sumimasen!” — call the staff; nobody comes until you do, and that’s polite
  • “Kore o kudasai” + pointing — fully legitimate ordering; menus and neighbors’ plates are fair game
  • “Okaikei onegaishimasu” — the check; pay at the register, never tip
